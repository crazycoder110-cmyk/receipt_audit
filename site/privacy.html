Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what invoice_audit ("the app", "we") does with your information. invoice_audit matches company card transactions to receipts found in your email and files them in your own Google Drive.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: crazycoder110@gmail.com.

Information we access

  • Google account email address (scopes openid, userinfo.email): to show which account is connected.
  • Gmail, read-only (scope gmail.readonly): to search your mailbox in the date range you choose for receipts and invoices, and to read those messages and their attachments. The app cannot send, change or delete email.
  • Google Drive, files created by the app (scope drive.file): to create a "Receipts" folder, save receipt files in it and keep a receipt log in Google Sheets. The app cannot see your other Drive files.
  • Card statement you upload: dates, descriptions and amounts of card transactions.
  • Fortnox (optional, only if you connect it): to upload matched receipts to your Fortnox inbox.

How we use it

Only to provide the features you see in the app: finding receipts, reading their merchant, date, amount and tax, matching them to your card transactions, and saving them to your Drive, your receipt log and, if connected, Fortnox. We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to build profiles.

AI processing

To understand receipts in any language, short extracts of likely receipt emails and attachments, and the transaction lines of your statement, are sent to our AI provider Groq, Inc. (United States) for processing. This is done only to return the extracted fields to the app.

Google user data and Limited Use

invoice_audit's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is not transferred to others except as needed to provide the features above (the AI processing described here), for security reasons, or to comply with law. No human reads your email unless you ask us to for support, or it is required for security or by law. Google user data is not used to train general-purpose AI models.

Storage and retention

We do not run a database. While you use the app, your session (including the sign-in token and results) is held in server memory and is deleted when you disconnect, or automatically after 6 hours of inactivity. Receipts and the receipt log are stored in your own Google Drive and stay under your control; Swedish bookkeeping rules usually require you to keep them for 7 years.

Your choices

  • Use "Disconnect and delete my data" in the app to revoke access and clear your session.
  • Remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data by writing to the contact address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
