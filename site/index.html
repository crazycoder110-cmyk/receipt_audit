The problem

Receipts are a small job that eats your week

Your accountant needs a receipt for every card payment. But the receipts are spread across your email, in different formats and languages. Finding them is slow, boring and easy to forget.

Receipts everywhere

Hotel bills, taxi rides, software subscriptions. Each lands in a different email, some as PDFs, some in the email text.

Month-end rush

At the end of the month you go through your inbox line by line, card statement in one hand, to find what belongs where.

Missing receipts cost money

Without a receipt you cannot deduct the VAT, and your accountant keeps asking. Small amounts add up over a year.

What invoice_audit does

An assistant that does the receipt work for you

Think of it as a careful assistant who reads your email every night, but is never allowed to send, change or delete anything.

  • Finds receipts and invoices in your Gmail, in any language and currency.
  • Matches each one to the right payment on your card statement, and says why.
  • Saves them in your own Google Drive, sorted by month, with a log in Google Sheets.
  • Asks you only about the few it cannot find: confirm, add a photo, or mark "no receipt".
  • Next: sends them straight to Fortnox for your accountant.
How it works

Three steps, about a minute

1

Connect your inbox

Sign in with Google. Email access is read-only, and receipts go to a folder in your own Drive.

2

Add your card statement

Drop the export from your card or bank: CSV, Excel or PDF. Mynt works out of the box.

3

Let the agent run

It finds, reads and matches the receipts, then shows you the few that need a tap from you.

Your data

How invoice_audit uses your Google data

Gmail, read-only

Used only to find receipts and invoices in the period you choose and read their amount, date, merchant and VAT. We never send, change or delete email.

Google Drive, app files only

Used to create a "Receipts" folder and a receipt log sheet. The app can only see files it created itself.

Nothing kept by us

No database. Your receipts live in your own Drive. Disconnect at any time and access is revoked.
