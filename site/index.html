AI receipt assistant

Every card payment, matched to its receipt.

invoice_audit reads your inbox, finds receipts and invoices in any language, matches them to your company card statement and files them in your own Google Drive with a log in Google Sheets. You only handle the few it cannot find.

Open the app How we use your data

How it works

1

Connect

Sign in with Google. Gmail access is read-only; receipts are saved in a folder in your own Drive.

2

Add your statement

Upload a card or bank export (CSV, Excel or PDF), for example from Mynt.

3

Run

The agent finds, reads and matches receipts, and asks you only about the ones it could not find.

How invoice_audit uses your Google data

Gmail, read-only

Used only to find receipts and invoices in the date range you choose and read their amount, date, merchant and tax. We never send, change or delete email.

Google Drive, app files only

Used to create a "Receipts" folder and a receipt log sheet. The app can only see files it created itself.

Nothing kept by us

No database. Your receipts live in your Drive. Disconnect at any time and access is revoked.

invoice_audit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Read the full privacy policy.
